Features

Everything between "it happened" and "it's handled."

Three pieces that work together: triggers that watch for events, logic that decides what happens next, and actions that carry it out — all visible, all editable.

Automate repetitive work

Turn the steps your team repeats every day into a flow that runs itself — approvals, hand-offs, status updates, reminders.

Connect your tools

See everything in one place

How it works

From idea to running automation in three steps.

01

Connect your tools

Link the apps you use — inbox, spreadsheet, CRM, chat — with a one-time sign-in. No API keys to manage.

02

Build the flow

Pick a trigger, add conditions, choose what happens next. Drag, drop, done — or start from a template.

03

Turn it on

Nimbus runs the flow every time the trigger fires, and shows you a full history of every run.
